The first tools for searching the internet indexed file names on FTP servers rather than page contents. As the web grew in the 1990s, full-text search engines began to crawl pages and index every word they contained.

Early engines ranked results mostly by how often query terms appeared, which made them easy to manipulate. Ranking by link analysis was a major step forward, and later engines added hundreds of signals, from freshness to the location of the searcher.
